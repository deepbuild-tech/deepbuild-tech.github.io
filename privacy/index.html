Privacy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you visit deepbuild.tech or email us. It is written in plain language. It covers this website only; each product will have its own privacy notice before it launches.

Who is responsible

This website is run by DeepBuild, Pune, India. Contact: hello@deepbuild.tech.

What this website collects

Nothing, directly. The site has no forms, accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising or third-party scripts. The only script is our own, for animation.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, as described in GitHub's documentation and the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive those logs.

If you email us

We receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation. Our email is provided by Google Workspace, which stores it for us. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else.

We keep emails for as long as we need them to deal with your message. You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@deepbuild.tech.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
