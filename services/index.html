Services

We give growing businesses the agentic systems that large companies build in-house: AI agents connected to their tools, carrying out real workflows, with people approving what matters. We build them, integrate them and keep them running.

Agentic workflows we build

  • Invoicing and collections

    Invoices raised from orders and delivery notes and sent on time; payments tracked, reminders prepared and receipts matched to your accounts.

  • Inventory and procurement

    Stock tracked against demand, reorders proposed before you run short, purchase orders drafted for approval and supplier follow-ups sent.

  • Customer support and enquiries

    Website, WhatsApp and email requests captured and routed together. AI can handle routine questions and order-status updates, with conversations passed to a person when they need judgement or care.

  • Lead generation and follow-up

    New leads found, recorded and followed up on time, so that interested customers are not lost between calls and messages.

  • Documents and data entry

    Purchase orders, bills, forms and reports read and entered into your systems, with anything unclear flagged for a person.

  • Scheduling and reminders

    Appointments booked and confirmed on WhatsApp, reminders and recalls sent, and missed appointments followed up.

  • Reporting and insight

    A regular summary for owners: what is growing, what is slowing down and what needs a decision.

  • Marketing

    Email, Instagram and other campaigns prepared and sent on a schedule, with the results recorded.

  • Web applications

    Fast, secure websites and web applications that your team and customers can rely on, owned by your business.

  • Ongoing care

    Monitoring, maintenance and improvements after launch, as the business and its tools change.

How an engagement runs

  1. Understand

    We map how the work is done today, and where time and customers are lost.

  2. Build

    We build the smallest system that removes the work, on building blocks we have already proven.

  3. Integrate

    We connect agents to the tools you already use: WhatsApp, email, your website, spreadsheets, accounting and billing software, and your inventory or practice-management system.

  4. Run and improve

    We maintain and extend it. Changes that affect your customers are reviewed by a person before they go live.

Your systems and data stay yours

Each client's application and data are kept separate from every other client's.

What we share across clients is know-how: proven ways to deploy, host and monitor systems. So each new project starts from work already done, without mixing one business's data with another's.

Our standard is to keep Indian clients' data in cloud regions in India.

Client: Tibbi Traders

Tibbi Traders supplies medical and laboratory consumables to hospitals, diagnostic centres, labs and distributors across Jharkhand and neighbouring regions.

DeepBuild is its technology partner. We built and run its web application and online catalogue at tibbitraders.com, and we built the agentic systems its team uses across sales and supply chain.

  • Enquiries and quotes

    Requests arrive by WhatsApp, phone, email and the website, and each needs prices from a catalogue of hundreds of products. An agent reads each request, matches the products, drafts the quote for the sales team to approve, and follows up.

  • Repeat orders

    Hospitals and labs reorder consumables on a cycle, and a missed reorder is a lost sale. An agent learns each buyer's ordering pattern and prompts the team before the buyer runs short.

  • Stock and replenishment

    High-turnover items have to be in stock for urgent orders. An agent tracks stock against demand and flags what to reorder from suppliers, and when.

  • Batches, expiry and documents

    Medical consumables carry batch numbers, expiry dates and documents such as certificates of analysis. An agent keeps them linked to every order, flags stock nearing expiry, and answers document requests.

  • Payments and follow-up

    Supplying institutions often means payment on credit. An agent tracks what is due and prepares reminders, so that collections do not depend on memory.

  • Business insight

    An agent prepares a regular summary for the owners: which products, customers and regions are growing, what is slowing down, and what needs attention.

The agents prepare, track and flag. People at Tibbi approve what goes to customers.
