Put AI agents to work in your business

Most AI answers questions. An agentic system takes the steps: it reads a request, checks your records, uses your tools, prepares the work and hands it to a person to approve.

Built into a business, that means invoices raised, stock reordered, enquiries answered and follow-ups sent, on time and on record.

  • Connected to WhatsApp, email, spreadsheets and your accounting and billing software
  • People approve anything customer-facing or that cannot be undone
  • Built on building blocks we have already proven, so it runs sooner

Questions businesses ask us

What is an agentic system?

AI agents connected to your tools that carry out the steps of a workflow: they read a request, check your records, use your tools, prepare the work and hand it to a person to approve.

Will the agents act without anyone checking?

No. Agents prepare, track and flag. A person approves anything that is customer-facing or cannot be undone.

Where is our data kept?

Each client's systems and data are kept separate from every other client's, and our standard is to keep Indian clients' data in cloud regions in India.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Growing businesses in India's tier-1 and tier-2 cities, in any industry where work runs on repeated steps: clinics and hospitals, distributors and suppliers, retailers, professional firms and service businesses.

How does an engagement start?

We map how the work is done today and where time and customers are lost, then build the smallest system that removes the work, connected to the tools you already use.

Can we use your products today?

Not yet. The AI assistant for CA firms is in development; the AI finance office and Apnon are in exploration. Email hello@deepbuild.tech if you would like to help shape them.
