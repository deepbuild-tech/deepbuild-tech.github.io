About DeepBuild

Our vision

Every business, not only the largest, should be able to put AI to work on its real problems.

Large companies already have teams building AI into how they work. We want the small and mid-sized businesses of India's tier-1 and tier-2 cities, the traders, accounting firms, clinics and service businesses, to have the same advantage: agentic systems built for their domain, their data and their rules, so that they can grow and run more efficiently.

Our mission

We design, build and run agentic systems for growing businesses, and we turn the problems we see repeated across industries into domain-specific agentic products, each designed to stand as its own business.

We are an engineering-led company. What we build is designed, tested, monitored and documented with the discipline of production platforms, because our customers' businesses run on it.

How DeepBuild is structured

DeepBuild is designed as an umbrella. Our services work with businesses across industries. Each venture product focuses on one domain and is designed to become its own business, with its own systems, its own data and, in time, its own company.

All of it is built on one DeepBuild foundation.

How we work

  • Outcomes over technology

    We judge a system by the time and errors it removes, not by the tools inside it.

  • Separate by design

    Each client and each product has its own systems and its own data.

  • People approve what matters

    Systems prepare and act; a person approves anything that is customer-facing or cannot be undone.

  • No dependence on one AI provider

    We choose the right model for each task and keep our systems portable.

  • Decisions in writing

    We record what we decide and why, so work can be checked and continued.

  • Reversible choices

    Where we can, we choose what is easy to undo.
